# glossary

Terminology slide decks for INFO 7375, Prompt Engineering for Generative AI, and a markdown glossary of every term in them.

- `decks/`: one self-contained HTML deck per chapter, named "NN Title.html" with NN the chapter number (01 to 15). Open in a browser; arrow keys or clicks step through the animations; press G for the slide grid. Same look as the course's review decks. A link ending in `#N` opens slide N.
- `GLOSSARY.md`: every term in every deck with its meaning and a tag (in the chapter, in the exam-term list only, outside source). Generated from the decks.

Each deck covers the chapter's most important terms (about 25 to 35), not every term in the exam-term lists; the lists in `decks/exam-terms/` stay the full inventory. Terms come from the chapter text; numbers on the slides are computed in the page or labelled as illustrative.
