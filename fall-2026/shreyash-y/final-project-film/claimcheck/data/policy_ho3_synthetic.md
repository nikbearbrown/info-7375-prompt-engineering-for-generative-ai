## Section 1 - Definitions

"You" and "your" mean the named insured shown in the Declarations.
"Residence premises" means the dwelling at the address shown in the Declarations.
"Sudden and accidental" means a loss that happens abruptly and is not expected or intended by you.

## Section 2 - Coverage A, Dwelling

We cover the dwelling on the residence premises, including structures attached to the dwelling.

## Section 3 - Coverage C, Personal Property

We cover personal property owned or used by you while it is anywhere in the world.

## Section 4 - Perils Insured Against

We insure for direct physical loss to the property described in Sections 2 and 3 caused by the following perils, unless the loss is excluded in Section 5.

4(a) Fire or smoke. Smoke means sudden and accidental damage from smoke, including the emission of smoke from a fireplace, but does not include smoke from agricultural smudging or industrial operations.

4(b) Water discharge. Sudden and accidental discharge or overflow of water from within a plumbing, heating or air conditioning system, including a pipe that bursts.

4(c) Windstorm or hail. Damage caused by windstorm or hail. The Windstorm or Hail Deductible shown in the Declarations applies to this peril instead of the All Perils Deductible.

## Section 5 - Exclusions

We do not insure loss caused by any of the following.

5(a) Flood. Flood, surface water, waves or overflow of a body of water, whether or not driven by wind.

5(b) Wear and tear. Wear and tear, deterioration, rust or mechanical breakdown.

5(c) Continuous seepage. Continuous or repeated seepage or leakage of water over a period of 14 days or more.

5(d) Water backup. Water that backs up through sewers or drains.

5(e) Mold. Mold, fungus or wet rot. This exclusion does not apply to mold that results from a loss covered under 4(b), if the mold is hidden within the walls or ceilings.

## Section 6 - Conditions

6(a) Notice of loss. You must give us notice of any loss within 60 days after the loss.

6(b) Proof of loss. You must send us a signed proof of loss within 60 days after we request one.

6(c) Other insurance. If other insurance also covers a loss, we pay only our share of the loss.

## Endorsement E-2 - Water Backup Coverage

This endorsement changes the policy. Exclusion 5(d) does not apply to the residence premises. We cover direct physical loss caused by water that backs up through sewers or drains, up to $10,000 per loss. The All Perils Deductible applies.
