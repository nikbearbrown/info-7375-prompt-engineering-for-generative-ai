  Figma is growing our team of passionate creatives and builders on a mission to make design accessible to all. Figma’s platform helps teams bring ideas to life—whether you're brainstorming, creating a prototype, translating designs into code, or iterating with AI. From idea to product, Figma empowers teams to streamline workflows, move faster, and work together in real time from anywhere in the world. If you're excited to shape the future of design and collaboration, join us!   
 Figma employees are among the most AI-active in the industry, and our Internal AI team, part of FTS (our Business Technology org), helps turn that energy into meaningful impact by making it easier for every Figmate, from Sales and Support to Finance, People, and Legal, to safely adopt AI and use it to improve how they work.  
 We’re a small, high-leverage team organized around three pillars: Platform & Tooling, Infrastructure & Hosting, and Applied AI Builds, and this role is a key member of the Applied AI Builds pillar. 
 As an AI Engineer, Internal Tools, you’ll work directly with teams across Figma to identify high-value AI opportunities and take them to production, moving from understanding a business workflow, to rapidly prototyping an approach, to evaluating it and integrating it with the systems and data needed to make it useful in the real world.  
 This isn’t a role focused only on building AI demos: you’ll own solutions through production, help determine where AI adds value and where traditional rules-based software is the right choice, and shape how agents can safely take actions across enterprise systems. As common patterns emerge, you’ll partner with our Platform & Tooling and Infrastructure teams to build reusable capabilities that make future AI builds faster and safer. 
 This is a full time role that can be held from one of our US hubs or remotely in the United States.  
  What you’ll do at Figma:  
 
 Partner directly with teams across GTM, Finance, People, Legal, Support, and other business functions to understand workflows and identify high-value opportunities for AI 
 Design, build, and productionize AI agents, assistants, and workflow automations that solve real business problems using LLMs, tool calling, RAG, structured outputs, agent frameworks, MCP, APIs, and enterprise data 
 Integrate AI applications with enterprise systems and data sources across GTM, Finance, People, Support, and internal APIs 
 Build evaluations and feedback loops to measure accuracy, reliability, and business impact before and after AI solutions reach production 
 Design deterministic guardrails, human-in-the-loop controls, and approval patterns for workflows where AI systems take business-critical actions 
 Build production-ready systems with strong observability, failure handling, security, authentication, authorization, and auditability 
 Identify reusable patterns across individual AI projects and build them out as shared components and capabilities in partnership with our Internal AI platform teams 
 Help define how Figma approaches Applied AI engineering and scales AI adoption across the company 
 
  We’d love to hear from you if you have:  
 
 5+ years of software engineering experience building production applications, integrations, backend systems, or AI-powered products 
 Hands-on experience building applications with LLMs, agents, retrieval-augmented generation, function calling, schema-constrained outputs, or evaluation frameworks 
 Experience taking an AI application or workflow beyond a prototype and operating it in a production environment 
 Strong API, integration, and system design fundamentals, with experience connecting applications across multiple systems and data sources and strong engineering judgment about when to apply AI and when a rules-based approach is the right choice, and experience designing reliable production systems with appropriate observability, failure handling, authentication, authorization, and security controls 
 Experience working directly with non-engineering stakeholders to understand ambiguous business problems and translate them into technical solutions and ability to move quickly from discovery to prototype while understanding what is required to safely and reliably productionize a solution 
 
  While it’s not required, it’s an added plus if you also have:  
 
 A background building internal AI applications or working in an Applied AI, Forward Deployed Engineering, or AI Solutions Engineering role 
 Experience with MCP, agent orchestration frameworks, and enterprise tool-calling patterns 
 Knowledge of AWS Bedrock, AgentCore, OpenAI, Anthropic, or similar AI platforms 
 Experience integrating enterprise systems such as Salesforce, Zendesk, Workday, NetSuite, Greenhouse, or Slack 
 Knowledge of with AI evaluations, observability, prompt/version management, and human-in-the-loop systems 
 Experience implementing enterprise identity and access patterns such as Okta, SSO, OAuth, and secure service-to-service authentication, within Security, Privacy, SOX/ITGC, or other enterprise governance requirements
