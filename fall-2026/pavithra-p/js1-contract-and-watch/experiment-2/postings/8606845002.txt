 AI Engineering at Brex is redefining how businesses run their finances by building intelligent, autonomous systems directly into the Brex platform. Our teams develop AI agents that don’t just surface insights—they take action, optimizing spend, managing workflows, and making real-time decisions on behalf of our customers.   What you’ll do  
 As an AI Engineer on our Finance Agents team, you’ll build Brex’s Agent Workbench: our singular conversational surface where finance professionals work with Brex’s agents through documents, charts, spreadsheets, and other interactive surfaces. You’ll help customers understand financial activity, investigate findings, revise policies, and steer agents doing consequential work. 
 Your first substantial project will be evolving the existing workbench into an extensible platform that can support multiple agent experiences, including bookkeeping and Brex Assistant. You’ll own the extension model, integration protocols, and application architecture that enable other teams to bring their agents and experiences into a coherent workspace. 
 You’ll establish the agentic design system with the technical depth to connect interaction design to platform decisions. Working closely with frontend engineers, agent teams, Product, and Design, you’ll build across application layers as needed and iterate with customers. The scope includes experiences such as policy editing, reporting, backtesting, and interactive data exploration, with priorities shaped by user needs. 
  Where you’ll work  
 This role will be based in our San Francisco office. We are a hybrid environment that combines the energy and connections of being in the office with the benefits and flexibility of working from home. We require three coordinated days in the office per week — Monday, Wednesday, and Thursday. As a perk, we also have up to four weeks per year of fully remote work! 
  Responsibilities  
 
 Design and build a reusable agent workbench platform that supports multiple agent personas and enables other teams to integrate their agents and product experiences.

 Own features from design through production and iteration: talk directly with customers, investigate failures, and use feedback and observed outcomes to improve how users understand, configure, and steer agents.
