 Reddit is a community of communities. It’s built on shared interests, passion, and trust, and is home to the most open and authentic conversations on the internet. Every day, Reddit users submit, vote, and comment on the topics they care most about. With 100,000+ active communities and approximately 130 million daily active unique visitors, Reddit is one of the internet’s largest sources of information. For more information, visit  www.redditinc.com . 
 
 
 
 
   The  Reddit Consumer Engineering  organization builds and scales the products that Redditors use directly to discover content, participate in communities, and connect with others. Sitting at the intersection of large-scale distributed systems and core user journeys, Product Backend Engineers in Consumer Engineering partner directly with Product Managers, Designers, Data Scientists, ML Engineers, and Frontend Engineers to build, experiment with, and scale features that power how hundreds of millions of users discover, join, navigate, and engage with Reddit. 
 This centralized role hires across several high-impact consumer teams, including: 
 
  Core Product & Feeds:  Home Experience, Recommendations & Content Discovery, Contribution 
  Community & Presence:  Reddit Pro, Youth Experience & Teen Safety, Creator Tools 
  Growth & Engagement:  Authentication, Notifications, Social Sharing, SEO, Onboarding. 
  Monetization & Conversion:  Paid User Acquisition, Conversion Funnel Optimization 
  Identity & Security:  Account Management, Trust & Safety 
 
  What You’ll Do  
 
  Product & Engineering Execution:  Partner closely with cross-functional partners in product, design, and platform engineering to execute on product strategy and build novel, user-centric media features. 
  Full-Lifecycle Development:  Drive the complete development lifecycle—from technical design and architectural proposals to implementation, unit/integration testing, experimentation, performance analysis, and deployment. 
  Quality & Velocity:  Move fast while maintaining high quality standards through thorough code reviews, detailed design doc evaluations, and constructive feedback on product specs and UI mocks. 
  Mentorship & Engineering Standards:  Help establish developer workflows, champion best practices, and mentor junior and mid-level engineers to elevate technical excellence across the team. 
 
   
  Who You Are  
 
  Product-Minded Engineer:  You bridge the gap between technical metrics and user experience, understanding how low-level optimizations impact real-world user engagement and product quality. 
  Pragmatic Builder:  You strike the right balance between rapid iteration and architectural rigor, delivering scalable solutions without getting bogged down by unnecessary processes. 
  Effective Collaborator:  You excel in communicating complex technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. 
  Continuous Learner:  You have a deep curiosity for emerging technologies and actively keep up with modern industry trends, standards, and performance optimizations. 
 
  Technical Qualifications  
 
  Frontend Expertise:  Strong proficiency in modern web stacks, including JavaScript, TypeScript, React, and Next.js, with a proven track record of translating complex designs into responsive, production-grade applications. 
  API & System Design:  Demonstrated experience designing, building, and maintaining robust APIs using protocols such as REST, GraphQL, gRPC, HTTP, or Thrift basics. 
  Education:  Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience.
