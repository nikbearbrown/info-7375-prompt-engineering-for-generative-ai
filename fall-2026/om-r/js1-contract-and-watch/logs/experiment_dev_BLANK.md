The team advises on architecture, runs technical evaluations such as proofs of value, and guides customers through digital transformation. We collaborate asynchronously with Sales, Product Management, Engineering, and Marketing to share effective practices, create reusable technical resources, and represent the voice of the customer. Our focus is helping customers modernize software delivery, adopt GitLab at scale, and navigate a changing AI and DevSecOps landscape.
