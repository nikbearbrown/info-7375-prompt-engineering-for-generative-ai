Staff Engineer, Revenue Experiences Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team The Revenue Experiences Engineering team is part of the Commerce Systems organization at Stripe. We focus on ensuring that our users, both internal and external, have a best-in-class experience with billing at Stripe. This includes working on the systems and processes needed to bill our largest and most complex customers, as well as designing and delivering experiences to help guide users through their billing, from fee explainability to deep diving on product usage optimization. We are a cross-functional technical team that seeks to bring clarity to a complex space. The team is at the crux of all Stripe products and builds user-facing as well as internal infrastructure. We build platforms and services to define and manage pricing, calculate and collect Stripe's revenue, and empower Stripe and our users to understand and manage revenue and costs. We work on customer-facing experiences, internal platforms, and data pipelines to empower Accounting, Finance, Strategy, Sales, and Stripe's Products to drive business value across the entire user and product lifecycle. What you’ll do We are seeking an experienced technical leader to join our India-based team and drive the technical roadmap for Revenue Experiences Engineering, collaborating deeply with partner teams across Commerce Systems in the US, EMEA, and India. In this role, you will work closely with engineering managers, senior engineers, and cross-functional partners to help design architecture, ensure technical excellence, and break down complex problems to make them more tractable with engineering teams. You will be involved in the building of the technical bench, mentoring and growing the next senior engineers within the broader organisation. You will work cross-functionally to align on the business challenges in delivering excellent billing and accounting experiences to our users. Often this will include influencing across organizations and grounding your arguments in solid data and a technical roadmap. As such, you will have excellent technical leadership and communication skills. You will also be hands-on in building key systems, while mentoring and leading other engineers. Join us in driving change in an area with tremendous opportunity. Responsibilities Manage the technical leadership, execution, and roadmap for Revenue Experiences Engineering. Work cross-functionally with business leaders across the company as well as partner engineering teams. Partner closely with engineering, product, and design leadership to define our product strategy and user experience. Ensure our platform solutions and user billing data are high quality, meet the needs of our internal users, and position us to meet Stripe's growth. Drive interoperability across platforms in User Billing and integrations across Stripe with product, accounting, sales, finance, and data science teams. Scrutinize billing interfaces and data models, elevating our focus on correctness and ease of use within User Billing. Establish best practices, frameworks, and repeatable processes that enable the team to execute effectively. Foster a culture of learning and data-driven decision making on the team and to influence the broader organization. Mentor and support the growth of engineers at different levels in their careers.
