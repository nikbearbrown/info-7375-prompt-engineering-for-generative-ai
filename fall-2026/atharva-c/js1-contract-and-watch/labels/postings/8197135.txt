Work on latency-critical, time-sensitive and data redundancy solutions. Enable the company to increase the GDP of the internet by providing uptime and data protection. Write code, scope, design, implement, and deploy solutions using multiple technologies, including: Ruby, Java, Mongo, Postgres, and other necessary tools as required. Learn, meet, and uphold high engineering standards through design and code review.
