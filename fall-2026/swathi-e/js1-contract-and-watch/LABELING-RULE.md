# LABELING-RULE.md — how I hand-label postings

Written by Swathi Baba Eswarappa, 2026-10-26, before labeling any posting.
Claude (chat) helped me put my four checks into words; I confirmed them and
added the last paragraph myself.

I read the full job page and mark it RELEVANT for Priya only if all four are true:
1. the job is AI/ML, data, or early-career software work,
2. it asks for 0-1 years of experience, or says new grad or entry level,
3. it does not need citizenship or a security clearance, and does not say it
   won't sponsor visas,
4. it is in the US or remote in the US.

If any one of these is false, I mark it NOT RELEVANT.

If the experience requirement isn't mentioned, I check the responsibilities
and skills to decide whether the role seems suitable for a recent graduate,
rather than automatically rejecting it.
