# Gaps — Atharva (fictional persona)

## Executive summary

This is the list of weaknesses a fictional job-seeker would admit to while searching, so demos and tests of the data-roles daily checker have a realistic person behind them. It shows where this persona is thin for the roles they target. The history and dates are invented; nothing here is a real person's record.

---

- **Experience:** one summer internship plus teaching-assistant work; postings asking for 3+ years of professional experience are a stretch.
- **Data engineering:** Snowflake and Airflow only from coursework — Data Engineer roles are a reach, Analytics Engineer roles are closer.
- **Clock:** the unemployment allowance runs out in mid-December, months before the OPT end date, so a slow hiring process can fail even at a company that sponsors.
- **Sponsorship:** needs an employer that both sponsors H-1B and is enrolled in E-Verify (for the STEM extension).
