# PROMPTS — "A Slogan Is a Division"

## Image / video generation prompts

**None.** This film contains no generated imagery of any kind: no text-to-image, no
text-to-video, no AI stills, no upscaled stock. Every frame is a deterministic render of
a registered Remotion composition over data printed by `research/llm_scale.py`.

Under brutalist.art's `nopunt` doctrine, a generated clip is never a legitimate way to
fill a beat, and under `docs/EXECUTABLE-EVIDENCE.md` a computable visual must be computed
rather than shopped for. Both apply to every beat here, so this file is empty by design
rather than by omission.

## Narration

All narration is local Kokoro (`am_onyx`), generated offline by
`runtime/scripts/generate_audio_kokoro.py`. No paid voice API, no cloning, no key.
Cost: $0.00.

## Claude transcripts

**None appear in the film.** See FACTCHECK.md → "Constructed vs. real".

The `ClaudeComposerAsk` frames in B00 and B07 render text I wrote. They are a title and
handoff device. They do not depict a conversation that occurred, and the film never
claims a model was asked or answered anything.
