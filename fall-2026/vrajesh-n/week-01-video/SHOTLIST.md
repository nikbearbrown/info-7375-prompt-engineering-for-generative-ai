# SHOTLIST — "A Slogan Is a Division"

9 beats. Every beat is a registered Remotion composition rendered by the pipeline.
**Zero slates, zero request cards, zero human media.** Nothing in this film needed a
photograph, so under `nopunt` doctrine there is no legitimate HOLD — everything animates.

Durations are MEASURED Kokoro narration (`mp3/timings.json`), not estimates.

| Beat | Act | Component | Owner | Measured | What is on screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B00 | ASK | `ClaudeComposerAsk` | pipeline | 11.95s | Cold open. The slogan typed as a question into the composer. Claude UI allowed here — cold open is an ILLUSTRATE LAW exemption. |
| B01 | BLUF | `BrutalistHesitantWriter` | pipeline | 10.71s | Executive summary. Types "The reading comparison is a **measurement**", hesitates, replaces the accented word with "**division**". The replaced word is the reel's actual misconception. |
| B02 | EVIDENCE | `FormACard` | pipeline | 19.84s | The three published GPT-3 figures, with arXiv:2005.14165 in the colophon tier. Light polarity. |
| B03 | MECHANISM | `TypesetMath` | pipeline | 17.54s | The division as real typeset notation — true fraction bar, units in the numerator and denominator. Row 1 at 0.0s, row 2 at 9.0s. Generated by `typeset_math.py`, never a text card imitating math. |
| B04 | MECHANISM | `ExecutedData` | pipeline | 25.55s | **The thesis beat.** Four reading rates reveal one at a time (1s/3s/5s/7s); the years column moves while nothing else does. Note line carries the spread and the execution provenance. |
| B05 | MECHANISM | `FormACard` | pipeline | 21.65s | The same move applied to the compute slogan. Two sentences that are both true and not the same sentence. |
| B06 | LIMIT | `FormACard` (`dark: true`) | pipeline | 22.63s | **What this does not establish.** The single dark beat — polarity punctuation, 1 of 9, inside the 70–80% light rule. |
| B07 | HANDOFF | `ClaudeComposerAsk` | pipeline | 10.62s | Your turn. A paste-able prompt, read aloud. Second and last appearance of the composer. |
| B08 | OUTRO | `FormACard` | pipeline | 6.36s | Neutral student outro: title restated, course, name. **No channel handle, no mascot, no jingle** — spoken, then a 1.0s silent tail. |

**Total measured narration: 146.85s (2:27).** Inside the assignment's 2–4 minute window
with no padding. The estimate in the beat sheet was 202s; the audio came in shorter, and
per audio-first doctrine the measured value wins and nothing was stretched to reach a number.

## Motion budget
One dominant mover per beat, never more: typing (B00, B01, B07), staged row reveal
(B03, B04), karaoke line reveal (B02, B05, B06, B08). No beat runs two languages of motion
at once, and no language covers more than ~40% of beats.

## Polarity
8 light / 1 dark (B06). Dark is used once, as punctuation on the boundary beat.

## Library-first (GATE L)
All five components were found with `./art scenes` before anything was authored; none were
invented for this film and none were slated.

```
./art scenes --check ClaudeComposerAsk BrutalistHesitantWriter FormACard TypesetMath ExecutedData

  BrutalistHesitantWriter  RENDERABLE  16:9  props: text, face, fontSize, lineSpacing, align, ink
  FormACard                RENDERABLE  16:9  props: lines, meta, dark
  TypesetMath              RENDERABLE  16:9  props: title, rows, note, conditions
  ExecutedData             RENDERABLE  16:9  props: title, mode, rows, note, columnLabels
```

`BarChart` was found and **rejected**: it is registered at 1280x720, and RENDER-TARGETS.md
requires code lanes to be born at 4K rather than upscaled. `ExecutedData` (1920x1080,
rendered at `--scale=2` to true 3840x2160) carries the same data honestly.
