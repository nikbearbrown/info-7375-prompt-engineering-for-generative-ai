# SHOTLIST: WorthLoop final project film

What is on screen in each beat. I decided what each beat shows; Claude
Code designs it with the Brutalist toolkit.

Rules for every beat:
- Labels (ILLUSTRATION, CONSTRUCTED, PLANNED, FICTIONAL) stay up for the
  whole beat, not a flash at the start.
- Every beat except B1 and B17 has a small part label, top left, up for
  the whole beat: "1 · What and why" (B2–B3), "2 · What others have done"
  (B4–B7), "3 · How I'm building it" (B8–B12), "4 · How I'll know it
  worked" (B13–B16).
- Things appear as the narration names them.
- Mechanisms move: B9, B11 and B16 are animated, not static.
- Company and project names are plain text, never logos.
- A claim from a source carries a small source line on screen.

B1  Opening card, up for the whole beat: the title "WorthLoop", and under
    it the tagline "Worth your time, or it doesn't make the loop." A small
    footer, up for all of B1: "Voice: synthetic (Kokoro) · Script: Prasad
    Shinde".
B2  ILLUSTRATION: a posting becomes a packet (posting, answers, why it
    fits); an Approve button, which the cursor clicks: the button presses
    in, fills with the accent color, and reads "Approved"; a "Never
    submits" stamp.
B3  The title "Why it matters" at the top. "≈254 applicants per job ad",
    source line "Greenhouse, via Fortune, Jul 2026"; applications pile up
    while the number counts up to 254.
B4  ILLUSTRATION, because the form is made up. Group 1, "Tools that apply

B6  Group 3, "Approval checks from AI payments": AP2's rule, paraphrased
