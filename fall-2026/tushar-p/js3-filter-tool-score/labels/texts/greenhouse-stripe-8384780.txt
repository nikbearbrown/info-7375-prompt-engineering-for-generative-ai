Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies, from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team The payments landscape is changing rapidly and our team is at the forefront of this change. Depending on where you live, you may be used to paying with a credit card or bank direct debit (United States), iDEAL (Netherlands) or UPI (India), amongst hundreds of other global payment methods. It turns out how you pay is increasingly dependent on where you are and what business you're buying from. The Core Payments team makes it easy for businesses global and domestic to offer familiar local payment experiences wherever their customers choose to pay. We build bank payment methods and local payment experiences, alongside the capabilities that make these methods easier to discover, integrate, and use across Stripe. Responsible for building Stripe's payment platform that allows us to build and distribute payment methods at scale. The team works across product, engineering, design, data science, partnerships, risk, and go-to-market teams to help businesses offer locally relevant payment experiences at global scale. What you’ll do As a Product Manager, you’ll own relationships with some of the world’s largest payment providers and build products that drive billions in payment volume growth. You’ll help merchants offer locally relevant payment methods online and in person, improve payment performance, and expand acceptance across borders and into use cases such as recurring and agentic payments. You’ll work directly with merchants to understand their payment needs, then partner with partnership managers and Stripe’s and financial partners’ product and engineering teams to define joint roadmaps and ship solutions. You’ll prioritize investments that grow adoption and improve approval rates while balancing revenue, margins, cost, and risk. Responsibilities Own the product vision and multi-quarter roadmap for a portfolio of strategic payment methods, using merchant feedback, partner capabilities, payment data, and market trends to set priorities. Work directly with merchants and financial partners to identify gaps in payment workflows and translate them into product requirements. Build joint roadmaps with partnership managers and partners’ product and engineering teams, securing alignment on investments and resolving competing priorities and dependencies. Make the case for new investments and work with engineering teams to ship improvements, balancing adoption, approval rates, revenue, margins, cost, and risk. Grow payment method adoption with go-to-market teams by expanding cross-border acceptance and supporting use cases such as recurring and agentic payments. Partner with Risk, Legal, and Compliance to implement controls and enforcement strategies that protect Stripe and financial partners while maximizing approval rates and minimizing losses. Work with product marketing and go-to-market teams to turn roadmap priorities into clear launch and adoption plans. Become the product expert for your payment partners, providing insights and support to help them succeed on Stripe.
