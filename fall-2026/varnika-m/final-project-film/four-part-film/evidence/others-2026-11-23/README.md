## What could not be verified

- LinkedIn's Premium "how you match" help page (`answer/a1462281`) returned HTTP 404 twice, so
  the film does not mention it.
- Neither the Jobright nor the LazyApply page says whether a person reviews an application before
  it is sent. The film says only what each page claims ("says its agent…").
- LazyApply's "1500 Applications/Day" is a plan limit, not a result, so the film does not use it.
- The Simplify pages are dated only "About 1 month ago", and the Indeed page has no date.
- These are company claims about their own products. Nobody tried the products for this film.
