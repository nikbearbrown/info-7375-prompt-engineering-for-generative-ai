# Labeling decisions — made before any label

My labels follow my JS1 labeling rule (`../../js1-contract-and-watch/experiment/LABELING-RULE.md`). Two questions came up while reading the first posting, before I had given any label. These are my decisions, made on 2026-11-25. Claude Code suggested both and I agreed: "ok" to each.

## 1. "3+ years"

**Decision:** "3+ years" as a must-have counts as **3 or fewer**, so it can be **Y**. "4+" or more is **N**.
**Why:** that is what my JS1 labeling rule says ("minimum years of experience being 3 or fewer").
**Known weakness:** Aarav has about 2.4 years, so "3+" asks for slightly more than he has. The audit reports this as a limit of the rule, and the labels do not hide it.

## 2. Security clearance, citizenship, visas

His résumé says nothing about citizenship, visas or work authorization. It is not decided from his name.

| The posting asks | Label | Reason written |
|---|---|---|
| Something he must **already have** that his résumé does not show: an **active** clearance with no "or willing to obtain", a license, an MBA or JD | **N** | "needs an active clearance he doesn't show" (or similar) |
| A **legal status** his résumé does not record: clearance **eligibility** or "willing to obtain", US citizenship, work authorization, "no visa sponsorship" | **Y**, if everything else fits | "status unknown" |

**Why:** the résumé is silent on his status, not negative about it. FILTER.md treats missing information as "cannot tell", not as a fail. Labels marked "status unknown" are counted separately in the audit.
