{"verdict": "relevant" or "not_relevant", "justification": [ ...one or more entries... ]}

- "posting_value": a short phrase copied from that posting field. Case and punctuation differences are ignored when it is checked, but its words must appear in that field in that order.

Cite the evidence that actually drove your verdict, whether it supports or argues against the match.
