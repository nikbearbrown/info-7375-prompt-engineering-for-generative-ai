## Entry 4 — 2026-11-23: building the four-part film, and Gate T at 1080p

**Facts**
- Script in the assignment's four parts (16 beats, 602 words, 4:04, Kokoro `am_michael`); every claim
  in `film/FACTCHECK.md`. H1BGrader and Interstride were found by Claude's web search, not by me, so the
  narration only says "I looked" for MyVisaJobs.
- I asked for 1080p, not 4K. `run.sh` always rendered scenes at 3840×2160 whatever `--height` said, so it
  was changed to render at `--height` (default still 4K). The submitted film was 1080p either way, which
  Claude only said clearly after I asked.
- Gate B stopped P4B once (a label 0.05 past the safe edge), fixed at the source. Gate V on the review
  cut: 32 frames, 0 blockers, 0 majors.
- **Gate T failed on 5 beats at 1080p.** Its pixel kerning test is skipped at 4K because it misfires, and
  at 1080p it measured rows through drawings and along letter bottoms. I chose "fix the checker".
  Claude's first fix passed all 34 real frames (this film and the pitch film), but **failed its own
  negative test**: text rendered with deliberately spaced letters also passed. So the fix was reverted;
  `type_check.py` is byte-identical to the original. Evidence: `film/_superseded/TYPECHECK-fail-1.md`.
- I then chose 4K scene renders with a 1080p film (as in the pitch film). `run.sh` was reverted too, so
  no gate or build script is changed. Gate T found one real issue at 4K: P3C box outlines read as a text
  blob over their labels. Fixed at the source (filled panels, no outline). Then Gate T **PASS**.
- Final: `film/sponsorscout-final-project-film.mp4`, 244.5 s, 1920×1080, sha256 `1c874b1c…8465624` (first build).
  Gate V on the captioned film 0/0; bookend PASS; listening check near-misses: "verdicts" heard as
  "vertex"; frame read: P1A "go nowhere" touches the pile. Full log: `film/_qc/gates-2026-11-23.txt`.
- **Two layout fixes after my review:** Claude's frame read found P1A's "go nowhere" touching the pile;
  I found P1B's arrow running over "network" while watching. Both fixed at the source and re-rendered;
  every gate passes again. New film sha256 `fd3ad7cf…206c0020f`; the first build is in `film/_superseded/P1A-P1B-v1/`.

**In my words:** *What I decided next, and why; what I checked when I watched the film? - The submitted film was 1080p anyway, I didn't even know that, therefore I thought I picked the quality from which it was to be output.

I fixed the checker because gate T's blunders were ‘false alarms': measuring by letter bottoms and drawings. It was easier to repair the broken check than to do something around it.

I also checked the film and realised that I was listening for the near misses, "to be", "verdicts" as "vertex".*
