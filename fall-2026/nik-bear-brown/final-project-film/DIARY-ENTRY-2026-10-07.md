## The diary rules

1. **One date, one film.** The opening line gives the date. The entry describes only what is true on that date.
2. **Past tense only for what exists and can be shown.** Everything else is "will." Nothing is rounded up.
3. **Every entry has the same five parts:** what exists, what ran, what didn't, what changed since the last entry, what's next.
4. **A failure goes in the same entry it happened.** It is never saved for a later one.
5. **Nothing is deleted or rewritten afterwards.** If an entry turns out to be wrong, the next entry says so.
6. **Short.** Two to four minutes, one idea per beat, pictures carry it.
