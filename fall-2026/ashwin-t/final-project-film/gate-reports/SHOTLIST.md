# Shot list — TraceCase final project film

Four-part structure set by the assignment: executive summary, introduction (prior work), methods,
evaluation. Every body beat is one drawn isometric Manim scene (show-tell), on a cream stage, with a
small heading naming which of the four parts it belongs to.

Cast kept for the whole film: the SERVICE (server stack; lights turn terracotta when it breaks),
LOG pages, the TRACE (span bars), the AGENT (magnifier), the CLAIM card, the GATE (barrier arm) and
the SEAL (approval stamp). Every picture of the unbuilt product carries the on-screen "constructed" tag.
