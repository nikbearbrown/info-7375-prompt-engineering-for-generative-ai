# Labeling protocol

Written by Ashwin Thankachan on 2026-10-31, after the split (seed 2608) and before
labeling any posting. Claude Code saved this text as given and wrote only this header.

The question each label answers: would Supriya Sharma, the fictional new-grad backend
engineer in `data/supriya-sharma.resume.json`, apply to this posting?

1. Role: Supriya would apply for backend, software engineering, full-stack, platform, infrastructure, and data engineering roles involving coding. SRE is acceptable if it involves software development and automation. Pure frontend, mobile development, and non-coding roles are not relevant.
2. Level: New Grad, Junior, Associate, Software Engineer I, and Engineer II are acceptable. Roles without a level are also considered if the experience requirements are reasonable. Senior, Staff, Principal, and Lead roles are excluded.
3. Experience: Roles asking for up to 2 years of experience are worth applying to. More than 2 years is a rejection, even if the skills match. A posting asking for “2+ years” counts as 2 years and is acceptable. Postings requiring “3+ years” or more are Not Relevant, even if the skills match.
4. Location: Boston, NYC, and US-remote positions are acceptable, including hybrid roles in Boston or NYC. Remote positions without a specified country are accepted only if the posting confirms US eligibility.
5. Skills: A posting should mention at least two relevant skills from Supriya's résumé. Equivalent names count, such as Postgres for PostgreSQL. General words like AI, cloud, or software alone do not count as skill matches.
6. Close calls: I lean toward Relevant when the role meets the main requirements but has minor uncertainty. However, a clear mismatch in seniority, experience, location, or job function means Not Relevant.
7. What I ignore: I will not consider salary, company reputation, perks, or benefits when labeling. I will also exclude visa sponsorship language from this experiment because I want to measure technical and career fit separately.
