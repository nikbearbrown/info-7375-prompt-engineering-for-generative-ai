Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team The Fraud Data Science team focuses on merchant fraud detection, account reviews, and interventions to protect Stripe and its users. We partner with Fraud Engineering and Risk Operations to turn data science into production systems and measurable improvements in fraud prevention. Our work includes evaluating how AI agents can support fraud investigations and review decisions while maintaining decision quality. What you’ll do We're looking for a Staff Data Scientist to help set the technical vision and roadmap for Fraud Data Science. This role combines hands-on technical leadership with cross-functional influence, with a particular focus on evaluating agentic approaches to merchant fraud detection and account reviews. You'll define how we measure the quality and impact of these approaches, translate findings into technical priorities, and align partners on where and how to apply them. You'll bring scientific rigor and practical judgment to decisions that balance fraud prevention, operational efficiency, and the experience of legitimate users. Responsibilities Shape the team's technical vision and roadmap across merchant fraud detection, account reviews, and interventions, identifying opportunities where data science can have the greatest impact. Lead cross-functional initiatives with Fraud Engineering, Risk Operations, Product, and other partners, turning ambiguous fraud problems into clear technical plans and measurable outcomes. Define evaluation frameworks for AI agents used in fraud investigations and account reviews, including evaluation datasets, quality metrics, offline testing, and online experiments. Combine model and agent evaluations with reviewer feedback and business outcomes to identify failure modes, guide iteration, and inform deployment decisions. Provide hands-on technical leadership in machine learning, statistical modeling, experimentation, and measurement, helping teams work through complex problems from research to production. Develop approaches to measure the impact of fraud interventions, accounting for tradeoffs among fraud losses, review efficiency, and friction for legitimate users. Translate technical findings into recommendations that influence priorities and investment across teams. Raise standards for evaluation, reproducibility, and production monitoring, and mentor data scientists in their technical and professional development. Lead complex projects from problem definition through modeling, implementation, and evaluation. Stay hands-on with analysis, code, and model development, and partner with engineering teams on production deployment. Establish strong practices for model evaluation, monitoring, reproducibility, and analytical rigor. Mentor data scientists and help peers grow their technical judgment, business understanding, and ability to deliver results.
