Who we are About Stripe Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies, from the world's largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups, use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone's reach while doing the most important work of your career. About the team Millions of businesses across the internet run on Stripe. The Stripe Business Network team is building the B2B network that turns that scale into value for every one of them: a place where businesses discover and transact with each other, share verified data, and move money instantly and seamlessly. We're building the experiences that make that network real. A single view of your entire Stripe network, including every platform, partner, and customer you do business with, where a new relationship is just a click away. Portable identity, so when you start working with a new platform, your verified business information comes with you instead of being collected from scratch. Closed-loop money movement, so paying another Stripe business is instant and costs a fraction of a card payment. A business directory where the right counterparties can find you. And trusted data sharing and profile representation, so counterparties have confidence in who they're working with. Every business that joins makes the network more valuable for everyone already on it. We think of the Business Network as the business graph for the internet economy, and we're building for what's next. What you'll do You will build the Stripe Business Network experiences, including the Network Tab (a single page to see your whole Stripe network), search and discovery, profiles, networked invoicing, and much more, to deliver utility to our users across the board. That means being a true champion of our users: end-to-end owner of core flows, a cross-functional partner to Engineering, Design, and GTM, and an active voice in shaping the Business Network's strategy alongside leadership. You'll define, build, and scale new and existing products, informed by user insights and data. We're looking for a technical PM who operates with a high degree of autonomy and drive. You care deeply about craft and impact. The only thing that really matters to you is getting well-built products into users' hands. You're already exploring how AI is changing how products get built and what it means for the PM role; you might even have a few half-finished side projects that prove it. Responsibilities Own the user's perspective. Spend time with users, and synthesize their feedback and market signals into clear product direction. Obsess over user impact rather than features shipped. Always ask (and quantify) how each release delivers value and utility to our users. Drive high-velocity, high-craft execution and own outcomes. Lead cross-functional work from discovery through launch, and hold yourself accountable for adoption, user value, and business impact. Own Stripe's strategic priorities. Use judgment to factor in Stripe's strategic positioning and leverage, and make network-accretive product choices. Partner deeply with Engineering, Design, Data Science, Marketing, Sales, and Operations to ship cohesive, frictionless experiences.
