Lever was founded eight years ago to tackle the most strategic challenge that companies face: how to recruit and hire top talent. We’re building the next generation of hiring software that companies like Netflix, Yelp, Cirque du Soleil, Shopify, and Eventbrite rely on to grow their teams. We've rethought the talent acquisition paradigm and are the innovation leaders in our space and looking for the right people to join us as we scale. We’re extraordinarily proud of the company we’ve built so far (not to mention humbled to be recognized as a Best Place to Work in San Francisco, as well as a top workplace in the entire United States). Our people are Lever’s biggest competitive advantage and we’ll continue investing in our “Leveroos” and people-first culture. THE CHALLENGE As Lever continues to grow our enterprise customer base, Lever Professional Services is looking for a high performing consultant to service the unique needs of these customers. Our implementation projects are often complex and global; involve a large customer team and executive stakeholders; and require process mastery and functional expertise. THE OPPORTUNITY Reporting to the Director of Professional Services, this is an incredible opportunity to work with Lever’s largest and most strategic customers, interact with global talent leaders and help redefine talent best practices at respected and well known brands. Internally, this is also an opportunity to contribute to the maturity of the Lever Professional Services team and development of Lever’s core product through customer feedback. THE TEAM You will be part of the Customer Experience Solutions (CXS) department at Lever which consists of Customer Success, Service & Support, Growth Implementation and Professional Services. We act as advocates for our customers by understanding their business challenges and collaborating with empathy to derive the most value out of our product while delivering a high quality customer experience. The Professional Services team provides an instrumental layer of success for enterprise customers through custom solutions, technical expertise, enterprise solution delivery and talent advisory.
THE SKILL SET
5+ years experience leading SaaS / software implementation project delivery for large complex enterprise customers Thorough understanding of SDLC and project management Growth mindset with a bias towards action Empathetic to customer and end-user needs, with the ability to translate those needs into solutions that deliver value Exceptional written, verbal and presentation skills with attention to detail Prior experience in Talent/HR/People related function strongly preferred
WITHIN 1 MONTH YOU'LL;
Attend Ramp Camp, Lever’s week-long version of onboarding. You’ll learn about all aspects of the business with a cross-functional group of new Leveroos. You’ll also get a chance during this week to kick off your first Lever project! Round out your onboarding during your second week focused on Lever’s go to market teams, by attending sessions hosted by Sales, Marketing, G&A and CXS. You’ll learn about Lever’s implementation offerings and service & support offerings, the Lever sales lifecycle, packaging and pricing, value drivers, contracts, statements of work, implementation methodology, launch process, and the Lever product roadmap. Shadow or review five (5) Implementation calls and training sessions across ENT customers to build an understanding of implementation approach, methodology, key milestones, systems, processes, and the differing customer needs by company size. Shadow or review three (3) each Customer Success Manager, Talent Analytics Consultant and Solutions Architect calls and across our Enterprise segment to build understanding of our closest cross-functional teams . Shadow an existing PS customer implementation to begin familiarizing yourself with existing PS delivery assets and the PS implementation process. Meet with SA lead to understand current competencies and solutions, backlog, and solution delivery process. Begin the journey towards Lever Product Certification through eight (8) immersive hands-on sessions with product certified Implementation Specialists. Meet with relevant teams both within the Customer Success organization and more broadly across the company. You’ll be working cross-functionally and building relationships early will set you up for success! Factor these learnings and observations into your 30-60-90-day plan which you will present to your hiring manager. This will set you up for success to continue to get to know your org, the business, and build bridges across the company.
