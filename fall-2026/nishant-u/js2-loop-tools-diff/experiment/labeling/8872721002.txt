About Faire
Faire is a technology wholesale platform built on the belief that the future is local. Independent retailers around the globe collectively represent a multi-hundred-billion-dollar wholesale market that has historically been fragmented and offline. At Faire, we're using the power of tech, data, and machine learning to connect this thriving community of entrepreneurs across the globe. Picture your favorite boutique in town — we help them discover the best products from around the world to sell in their stores. With the right tools and insights, we believe that we can level the playing field so businesses can grow and local communities can thrive.
We’re looking for smart, resourceful and passionate people to join us as we power the shop local movement. If you believe in community, come join ours.
About this Role
We are looking for a Head of Paid Marketing to drive efficient customer acquisition and retargeting at scale, in service of Faire's ambitious global growth goals on both sides of the retailer and brand marketplace. This is a hands-on leadership role: you will own the strategy, budget, and measurement for all of Faire's paid channels (including search, social, affiliates, display, audio/podcasts, video/CTV), act as the primary executive stakeholder for paid performance, and lead a dedicated team of paid marketers. The ideal candidate is a data-driven operator who has scaled 8-9 figure paid budgets in a two-sided marketplace or in support of small business owners, has hired and led high-performing, AI-native growth marketing teams, and is fluent in modern measurement/attribution methodology.
What you'll do
Own and scale global paid acquisition across both sides of the marketplace (retailers and brands) to $xxM annually while hitting target payback/efficiency thresholds; act as the primary executive stakeholder for paid performance
Define and communicate paid's long-term strategic roadmap to executive stakeholders, and build a resourcing and investment framework to address key risks such as channel consolidation, global competition, customer TAM, and brand awareness
Build a best-in-class retargeting/remarketing program to drive incremental marketplace GMV and re-engage lapsed retailers and brands
Diversify the paid channel mix by testing and scaling new channels beyond today's core, with clear incrementality evidence and Finance & Strategy alignment
Build the measurement and attribution infrastructure (incrementality testing, MMM, multi-touch attribution) needed to make confident investment decisions in upper-funnel and brand media
Hire, coach, and retain a paid marketing team recognized as best-in-class; establish AI practices — including self-serve workflows — that transform and automate our core paid marketing processes to compound experimentation velocity and decision quality
Partner closely with Creative and Product leads to build a high-velocity A/B testing process spanning creative messaging, customer needs, and user segmentation into a unified experimentation roadmap
Partner with Engineering leadership to harden the data pipelines paid depends on (tracking, tagging, feeds, attribution); audit and onboard external vendors/agencies that strengthen the MarTech stack and unblock team velocity
Qualifications
12+ years of experience in performance/paid marketing, with deep, hands-on expertise across paid search, paid social, affiliates, display, and CTV/video
Track record scaling paid acquisition budgets (8-9 figures annually) while hitting efficiency/payback targets in a fast-growing company
Superb written and oral communication skills; able to represent performance, risk, and opportunity crisply to senior leadership
Proven track record partnering with creative and product teams to build a high-velocity, A/B testing culture across core and experimental channels
Proven people leader who has hired, developed, and scaled a high-performing paid marketing team; comfortable operating as both strategic leader and hands-on operator
Fluent in measurement/attribution methodology (incrementality testing, MMM, multi-touch attribution) and comfortable partnering with data science/analytics on experiment design
Experience using AI to automate and scale paid marketing testing and operations (e.g., creative testing, audience testing, bidding signal testing, keyword signal testing) — treats this as how the team runs day-to-day, not a nice-to-have
Experience evaluating, onboarding, and managing external MarTech vendors or agencies
Exemplary budget ownership, with strong partnership with Finance teams to define forecasting and performance narrative across multiple channels and audience segments
Comfortable operating with ambiguity in a fast-growing, evolving organization
Excited and authentically motivated by Faire's mission
Genuine, empathetic, humble, and kind team player and cross-functional collaborator
Bonus points
Experience leading brand awareness or upper-funnel campaigns, comfortable making the case for expanded investment with senior stakeholders
Experience in two-sided marketplaces or with products aimed at small business owners
Experience leading international/global paid marketing at a public company
Strong opinions on creative messaging and brand aesthetic; opinionated about when to use AI for creative experimentation in paid contexts
Experience partnering with Engineering to harden data pipelines and tracking infrastructure that paid marketing depends on
