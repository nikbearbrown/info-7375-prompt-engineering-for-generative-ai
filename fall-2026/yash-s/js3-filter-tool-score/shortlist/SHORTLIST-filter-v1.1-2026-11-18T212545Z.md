## How each claim is labeled

| Claim kind | Rule the checker applies |
|---|---|
| title | supported: the words appear in the title, which is what Rule 1 reads |
| mention | supported: the phrase appears word for word in the job-specific text |
| work | supported only if a sentence with one of the category's phrases sits under a requirement or responsibility heading; otherwise insufficient. A mention under a company, team, preferred or unclear heading does not establish that the role does this work |
| skill-named | supported: the skill appears word for word in the job-specific text |
| skill-required | supported only if a sentence naming the skill sits under a requirement heading and has no preferred word; otherwise insufficient |
| location | supported if a location part is US by Rule 7; insufficient if unknown; contradicted if every part is outside the US |
| experience | from Rule 8: compatible -> supported; a mandatory minimum above eight months -> contradicted; unknown -> insufficient, and the agent says "I cannot tell" |
| early-career | supported only by an explicit early-career statement; contradicted by a seniority exclusion or a mandatory minimum above the cutoff; otherwise insufficient, with any experienced-candidate wording quoted (Anthropic's standard experience sentence is not counted as such wording) |

## The abstention rule and a counterexample

**Rule (FILTER.md section 6):** when the posting does not state a mandatory year count, or states one that cannot be read reliably, the agent answers "I cannot tell" about experience, labels the experience claim insufficient, and an otherwise promising posting (score at least 65) goes to the review queue instead of the confirmed shortlist.

**How often it fires:** 63 of the 63 review-queue postings are there because experience is unknown. On Anthropic, 351 of 677 postings have unknown experience, partly because every Anthropic posting carries the same sentence, "Years of experience required will correlate with the internal job level requirements for the position", which Rule 8 sets aside.

**Counterexample:** the #1 review candidate, Data Engineer, GTM Systems (Anthropic `5464234008`, score 90). The rule abstains, correctly: there is no mandatory year count. But the posting says: Preferred, not required: "5+ years of experience in data engineering, analytics engineering, or GTM data roles" (under the heading 'Preferred qualifications'). The abstention avoids a false claim that Priya is eligible, yet the ranking still puts this posting first, above postings with no evidence against Priya's fit. "I cannot tell" is treated as neutral when the text actually leans one way. The rule protects against a false *yes*; it does nothing to stop a likely *no* from being ranked first.
