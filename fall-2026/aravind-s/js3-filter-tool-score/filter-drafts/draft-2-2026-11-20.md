New Topic Words: Description-Based Ranking Signals
Search the job description for these technical terms. A word only adds points when it appears in an engineering context, such as building, developing, designing, implementing, maintaining or operating software.

* Backend: backend, back-end, server-side, server side. Do not match general back-office work.
* APIs: API, APIs, REST, RESTful, GraphQL, gRPC, endpoints. Do not match general API mentions in sales or customer support.
* Distributed systems: distributed systems, distributed services, event-driven architecture, message queues, Kafka. Do not match references to employees working in different locations.
* Databases: SQL, PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, Redis, database schema, query optimization. Do not match general mentions of customer databases or data entry.
* Cloud infrastructure: AWS, Amazon Web Services, GCP, Google Cloud, Azure, Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, cloud infrastructure. Do not match general cloud-storage references.
* Microservices: microservice, microservices, service-oriented architecture. Do not match unrelated customer services.
* Developer tools: developer tools, developer platform, internal developer platform, SDK, CLI, build systems, CI/CD, developer experience. Do not match general business platforms.

These are ranking signals, not automatic proof that a job is relevant. The reject audit will help identify words that match for the wrong reasons.

How the Ranking Works
Every posting that passes the hard rules starts with 50 points.

* Add 5 points for each different technical topic group found in the description.
* Topic points are capped at 25 points.
* Subtract 15 points for US locations outside Jane's preferred areas.
* Subtract 10 points for a missing or unclear location.
* Subtract 10 points for a missing employment type.

The minimum shortlist score is 60 points. The agent will sort postings from highest to lowest score and select the top 10 postings that meet the minimum. If fewer than 10 meet the cutoff, it will return fewer.
If two postings have the same score, prefer the one with more technical topic matches, followed by the one in Jane's preferred location.
These starting weights and the cutoff are hand-set for version 1.0. They are not trained or fitted using model data. I will use the audit results and held-out labels to decide whether these numbers need to change.
