## What this film does not claim

It does not claim the tool exists, that anyone has used it, that it catches any particular
share of errors, or that any named product is bad at its own job. Rider, the Microsoft
analyzers and Project Auditor are described doing what their documentation says they do; the
film's point is that none of them answers this particular question, not that they are wrong.
It does not claim Unity is silent about every wrong string — row 4 exists because it is not.
And it does not claim that no such checker exists anywhere, only that the author looked and
did not find one — row 18.
