We are looking for an Account Executive, Public Sector - State & Local to drive new business and expand Figma’s presence across State & Local government agencies. As the first dedicated State & Local Account Executive at Figma, you will help define Figma’s initial go-to-market approach for State & Local government, establishing the strategies, process, and best practices that will guide future sales hires.
This role requires proactive pipeline generation, strategic account management, and the ability to navigate complex State & Local buying processes. You will work closely with Design, Product, Engineering, and IT leaders to understand their priorities, demonstrate Figma’s value, and grow adoption across their organizations. This is an opportunity to help shape Figma’s State & Local sales strategy, learn from leading sellers, and build a strong network of cross-functional partners.
This is a full-time role that can be held from our San Francisco hub or remotely within the Pacific US time zone.
What you’ll do at Figma:
Own a named book of State & Local accounts, driving acquisition, expansion, and retention to exceed quarterly and annual sales targets
Build pipeline through outbound prospecting, territory planning, account mapping, partner collaboration, and public sector engagement
Develop strategic account plans aligned with agency priorities, funding sources, budget cycles, and purchasing timelines
Lead multi-stakeholder sales cycles, building executive sponsorship and internal champions while navigating security, accessibility, and SLED procurement requirements
Partner with Solutions Engineering, Customer Success, Marketing, and channel teams to demonstrate value, advance opportunities, and drive adoption
Win foundational government customers, establishing reference accounts and success stories that define Figma’s value across State & Local government
Own RFP strategy and proposal execution, mobilizing internal stakeholders under tight deadlines to win competitive government bids
Make accessible citizen experiences central to the value story, showing how Figma helps agencies modernize digital service delivery and better serve their communities
We'd love to hear from you if you have:
8+ years of enterprise sales experience, including significant SaaS experience selling into State & Local government agencies
Consistent success acquiring new accounts, expanding existing relationships, and exceeding sales targets, supported by proactive outbound prospecting and strategic territory development
Experience managing complex sales cycles of six months or longer, with an understanding of SL budget cycles, procurement requirements, and reseller and channel partner ecosystems
Demonstrated ability to build trust with executives, technical teams, and agency or institutional leaders, aligning solutions to organizational priorities through effective discovery, qualification, and value-selling
Strong communication, negotiation, and account planning skills, with a structured sales methodology and the ability to manage competing priorities across a territory
While it’s not required, it’s an added plus if you also have:
Established relationships within the assigned SL territory and experience co-selling with resellers, systems integrators, or other public sector partners
Understanding of public sector requirements for data privacy, accessibility, and security
Demonstrated success in a changing environment or building an emerging sales motion, with certification or formal training in deal qualification and prospect discovery
