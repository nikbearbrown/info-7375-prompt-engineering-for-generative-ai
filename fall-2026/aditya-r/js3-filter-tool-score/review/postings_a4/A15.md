Figma is growing our team of passionate creatives and builders on a mission to make design accessible to all. Figma’s platform helps teams bring ideas to life—whether you're brainstorming, creating a prototype, translating designs into code, or iterating with AI. From idea to product, Figma empowers teams to streamline workflows, move faster, and work together in real time from anywhere in the world. If you're excited to shape the future of design and collaboration, join us!
At Figma, our Solutions Consulting team bridges technical expertise with business context to help customers realize what’s possible with our platform. As a Solutions Consultant supporting Australian accounts, you’ll partner with Sales and cross-functional teams to craft and deliver consultative strategies that showcase Figma’s value. You’ll lead technical discussions, demos, and solution design to help customers achieve their goals and transform how they build products, playing a key role in driving revenue and growth as Figma continues to innovate
What you’ll do at Figma:
Paint the vision of the Figma Platform and guide customers in realizing the value through product demonstrations and presentations
Support the Sales team by addressing Figma platform questions and showcasing how Figma accelerates digital product delivery to customers
Own the customer IT and Security relationship during the deal cycle by addressing security questionnaires and enterprise requirements, in order to help customers adopt the platform securely and at scale
Collaborate with Product and Support to identify opportunities for enhancing account security through new product features and help influence the product roadmap
Design and scale technical sales efforts, facilitating conversations and connections between our customers, sales and product teams
Create thoughtful content to delight customers and internal partners surrounding the Figma platform
Collaborate with amazing Figmates to hone your craft and execute daily, we are looking for fantastic life-long learners who can operate with autonomy
We'd love to hear from you if you have:
8+ years professional experience, including 4+ years of sales engineering or solution consulting experience in a SaaS organization
Proven ability to quickly learn new technologies and effectively position solutions in presales, including defining success criteria and leading evaluations
Strong communication skills with experience delivering compelling presentations and product demonstrations to a variety of audiences, understanding and addressing the variety of personas and workflows across Product, Design, and Engineering
Experience with front-end development or the developer lifecycle
While not required, it’s an added plus if you also have:
Experience using or working with Figma, or other UX/UI experience
You embody our values and can thrive in a constantly evolving and growing environment
