P-301
As a Candidate Experience Admin at Databricks, you'll be the engine that powers our in person recruiting efforts, focusing on physical logistics and execution to deliver a smooth, error-free onsite candidate experience. As one of the primary points of contact for candidates visiting our offices, you'll partner with Recruiters, Sourcers, and hiring teams to deliver an exceptional, high-touch interviewing experience.
The impact you will have:
In person in one of our hubs: San Francisco or Mountain View office, 5 days a week
Serve as the primary onsite point of contact for candidates, managing timely, professional, and friendly communication (written and verbal) to ensure candidates feel supported throughout their journey
Build relationships and trust with the broader Talent Acquisition team, cross-functional partners, and hiring stakeholders
Manage physical logistics and in person candidate experience, maintaining an active day-of presence to oversee candidate greetings, manage room changes, and perform live troubleshooting
Coordinate interviews, prioritizing high-touch, time-sensitive scheduling needs, and hiring experience
Contribute to and identify data-driven improvements and ad hoc projects that enhance our recruitment processes and candidate experience programs
What we look for:
Active hands-on logistics management and real-time troubleshooting capabilities
Effective communication and prioritization skills
The ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment with evolving in person and scheduling needs
Collaborative mindset and openness to feedback
Proactive approach to resolving ambiguous challenges and unexpected in person logistics on the fly
A track record of high accuracy and attention to detail
An interest in technology and artificial intelligence
