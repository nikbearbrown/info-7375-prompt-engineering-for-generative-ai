# PROMPTS — Fine Print (pitch film)

**No generation prompts.** Nothing in this film was made by an image, video or music model.
Every picture is drawn in code (Manim scenes in `scenes.py`, Remotion bookends from the toolkit), and the narration is
synthesized locally by Kokoro (`am_eric`) from the script in `beat_sheet.json`. The prompts I gave Claude Code to build
the film are in `BUILD-PROMPT.md`.
