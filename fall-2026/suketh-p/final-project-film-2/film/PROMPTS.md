# PROMPTS — Fine Print (final project film, DRAFT)

**No generation prompts.** Nothing in this film was made by an image, video or music model. Every picture is drawn in
code: the Manim scenes in `scenes.py` (assembled by `build_scenes.py` from the toolkit's `iso_kit.py`, its 25 approved
props, the generated `scene_data.py` and this film's `scenes_body.py`), and the toolkit's Remotion hesitant-writer
bookend for BIDEA. The terminal frames in B08, B09 and B10 replay saved logs from `../evidence/terminal/`; they are
not screenshots and nothing in them was typed by hand. The narration is synthesized locally by Kokoro (`am_eric`) from
the script in `make_sheet.py` / `beat_sheet.json`.
