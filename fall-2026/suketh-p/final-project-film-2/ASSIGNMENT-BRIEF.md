Final Project Film

* Due Monday by 11:30pm
* Points 25
* Submitting a text entry box, a website url, a media recording, or a file upload

Subject: What your project film needs to show
Hi everyone,
You’re each making a film about your project. It needs to be crystal clear. Build it in these four parts, in this order.
1. Executive summary: what are you doing, and why would anybody care?
Open with what your project is, in plain words: what you’re building and why it matters. Keep the “how” out of this part. If someone watches only the first minute, they should know what you’re doing and why it’s worth doing.
2. Introduction: what have other people done?
Briefly, what already exists? Who has tried this or something like it, and what did they get? Then say again why anybody should care about yours.
3. Methods: how are you going to do it?
This is your plan. How are you going to build what you’re building? Where is your data coming from? What exactly are you going to do, step by step? Do you have what you need: the token credits, the accounts, the access, the compute? Keep the detail here and in your appendix, never in the executive summary.
4. Evaluation: how will you know it did a good job?
How are you going to evaluate it? What will you measure or check, and what result would tell you it worked, or that it didn’t?
To sum up: what you’re doing and why it matters, what others have done, how you’re going to do it, and how you’ll know it worked.
Professor Bear
