## 2. Relevance is not entailment

**Figma, "Software Engineer - Distributed Systems" (`greenhouse/figma/5552549004`).** It has the
highest possible score, 9: a role phrase in the title (R1), three résumé skills (R4: TypeScript,
Python, Redis, Kafka, AWS, GCP appear; capped at 3), a topic word (R5) and a US location (R6).
Every word the filter looks for is there. Yet the claim "Kiran's experience meets the posting's
stated minimum" is **contradicted** by the posting itself: "5+ years of Software Engineering
experience, specifically in backend…". The score measures how much of the posting's vocabulary
overlaps with Kiran's; it does not establish that the posting supports the claim that Kiran fits.

The same gap sits inside our own labeller. C2 is labelled "supported" whenever the skill word
appears, but on Ramp's "Backend Engineer, Ops Engineering" the words appear as "programming
languages **such as** Python, TypeScript/Node.js, **or similar**". The posting names Python as an
example, not as a requirement. "Asks for Python" is relevant to that sentence, and not entailed by
it. All 172 "supported" C2 labels are word matches, read with that limit.

## 3. The abstention rule and a counterexample

**The rule (FILTER.md §2).** For any claim with no span either way, the agent writes "I cannot
tell". If C3, C4 or C5 is insufficient, it does not say the posting fits ("matches the filter; I
cannot tell whether it fits on: …"). Under 200 characters of text, it cannot tell anything but the
title. A contradicted claim is said first.

**Counterexample: Stripe, "Software Engineer, New Grad" (`greenhouse/stripe/8128744`).** The agent
answers "Matches the filter; I cannot tell whether it fits on: C4, C5". On C4 it abstains, because
no sentence states a minimum number of years. But the text does decide C4, against Kiran:

> New grad candidates should have no more than 18 months of professional experience (excluding
> internship experience).

Kiran has four years. A human reading the posting can tell; the rule cannot, because it only looks
for a *minimum* ("N+ years") and this sentence states a *maximum* in months. The abstention is
not caution here, it hides a contradiction. The rule's limit: **it abstains when the evidence is
in a form it was not written to read, and it cannot tell that apart from real silence.** This
posting also scored 8 under the filter and was labelled MATCH under the JS1 rule
(`experiments/prompt/labels/dev.labels.json`): every layer missed it the same way. Claude, given
the tool's output through MCP, repeated the abstention ([TOOL_SERVER.md](TOOL_SERVER.md) §2).

Two more of the same kind, for the record:

- **Abstains though the text decides:** Stripe "Software Engineer" (`8249901`) says "plus three (3)
  years of software engineering or related experience". The `(3)` between the number and "years"
  breaks the pattern, so C4 is insufficient though the text states three years (which Kiran meets).
- **Answers though it should hedge:** Ramp "Software Engineer, Frontend" says "Minimum of 5 years
  of frontend engineering experience **preferred**". The rule labels C4 contradicted as if five
  years were required; the posting only prefers it.

Both limits are pinned by tests (`tests/js3/test_claims.py::test_limit_*`), so fixing them would
be a visible change to FILTER.md §2, not a silent one.
