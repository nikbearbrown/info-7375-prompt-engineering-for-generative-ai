Who we are
About Stripe
Stripe is a financial infrastructure platform for businesses. Millions of companies—from the world’s largest enterprises to the most ambitious startups—use Stripe to accept payments, grow their revenue, and accelerate new business opportunities. Our mission is to increase the GDP of the internet, and we have a staggering amount of work ahead. That means you have an unprecedented opportunity to put the global economy within everyone’s reach while doing the most important work of your career.
About the team
The administrative team works with Stripe’s leaders to support their day-to-day administrative and operational needs.
What you’ll do
In this role, you’ll play a critical part in supporting our business function.
Responsibilities
Provide complex, proactive calendar support for multiple leaders
Make recommendations to your leaders about time management, prioritization, delegation, and organization
Coordinate domestic and international travel and manage all related details
Prepare and submit expense reports on behalf of your leaders
Who you are
We’re looking for someone who meets the minimum requirements to be considered for the role. If you meet these requirements, you are encouraged to apply. The preferred qualifications are a bonus, not a requirement.
Minimum requirements
1+ years of administrative experience
Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and ability to execute
Strong time-management skills, with the ability to adjust tasks and priorities as needed
Ability to work across roles and teams in a fast-paced, changing environment while remaining flexible, proactive, resourceful, and efficient
Ability to communicate and perform well under pressure or ambiguity, anticipate the needs of others, and handle sensitive information and situations with care and confidence
Business proficiency in written and spoken English to communicate with leaders and coordinate calendars, travel, and other administrative needs
Preferred qualifications
Experience with Google Workspace
Experience supporting multiple complex calendars
Experience booking and managing domestic and international travel
Experience preparing expense reports
