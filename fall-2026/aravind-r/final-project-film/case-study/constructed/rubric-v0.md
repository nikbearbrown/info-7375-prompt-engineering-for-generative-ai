# Rubric v0 — Meeting Recap

Each clause is judged **pass** or **fail** for one recap against its meeting transcript. This is version 0: it is written the way a PM would write a first rubric, and it has not been tuned against any judge.

| ID | Clause | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Every decision listed in the AMI human summary's DECISIONS section appears in the recap in substance (paraphrase is fine). Reference: that meeting's summary.txt. | objective-leaning |
| C2 | **Claims trace to the transcript.** Every decision and action item points to a transcript line that supports it. | objective-leaning |
| C3 | **Action items have owners.** Each action item names the role responsible, or says "owner not assigned" when the meeting gave none. | objective-leaning |
| C4 | **Unresolved stays unresolved.** Disagreements and open questions the team didn't settle are reported as unresolved, not as decisions. | subjective |
| C5 | **Useful to someone who missed it.** A teammate who wasn't there could act on the recap without reading the transcript. | subjective |

A recap's result is the five pass/fail values. There is no overall score.
