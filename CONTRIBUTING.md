# Contributing

Read AGENTS.md. Keep the course Claude-only and Python-only, with standard-library implementations and explicit limits. Every new lesson needs a from-scratch build, applied Claude exercise, shipped artifact, six-question quiz, and at least five meaningful tests.

Run python3 scripts/validate_course.py. Preserve the original MIT notice and source attribution. Product claims require dated official documentation. Do not turn a teaching fixture into a purported live result or silently settle instructor policy conflicts.
