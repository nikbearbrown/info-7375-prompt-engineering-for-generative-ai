# Frictional — 10 points

**Course AI policy:** [AI Policy for Professor Bear's Courses | Using AI Responsibly in Class](https://youtu.be/8Ut0Cdl6vMw?si=9w3aEpt1ZyAR4Kiz).

An honest log of effort for each graded Assignment. Your artifact shows what you produced; Frictional records what you tried, questioned, checked, and learned. It is not a polished essay, a timesheet, or a performance of suffering.

Keep dated entries in `FRICTIONAL.md` as you work. Short bullets are enough. Label retrospective entries honestly; do not fabricate timestamps, difficulties, or history.

| Criterion | Points | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Your attempts | 2 | Specific things you actually tried and what you expected. |
| Friction and response | 2 | A confusion, unexpected result, difficult decision, or assumption checked, and your response. Do not invent difficulty. |
| Human and AI contributions | 2 | What you did, what Claude or another person contributed, and what you accepted, changed, rejected, or still do not understand. |
| Learning and uncertainty | 2 | What changed in your understanding, or what remains unresolved and your next question or step. |
| Traceable process | 2 | Dated entries connected to relevant commits, prompt excerpts, outputs, drafts, or tests. Complete chat transcripts are unnecessary. |
| **Total** | **10** | |

Each row: 2 = specific and sufficient, 1 = vague or incomplete, 0 = absent. An unsuccessful attempt can earn full credit. More hours, mistakes, commits, or polished prose do not earn extra points. If the work was straightforward, say so and describe what you checked.
