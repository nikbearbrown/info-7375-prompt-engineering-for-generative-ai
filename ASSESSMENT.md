# Assessments and graded Assignments

**Course AI policy:** [AI Policy for Professor Bear's Courses | Using AI Responsibly in Class](https://youtu.be/8Ut0Cdl6vMw?si=9w3aEpt1ZyAR4Kiz).

**Assessments are ungraded practice. Assignments are graded submissions, worth 100 points each, due every 10 days.** The fifteen-week lesson sequence is a learning sequence, not fifteen weekly graded assignments.

## Ungraded Assessments

The existing lesson exercises, builds, practice labs, knowledge checks, and quizzes are Assessments. Use them to predict, implement, test, explain, and learn before preparing a graded Assignment. Automated tests and quiz answers give feedback, not academic grades. Reference solutions remain references, not your submission.

## Graded Assignments: every 10 days

Each Assignment identifies the Assessments to implement and the expected scope. Submit your own Python implementation, reproducible commands, results, and meaningful tests, including at least two additional tests beyond the references. Explain the mechanism and distinguish actual Claude runs from offline fixtures or simulations.

| Component | Points | What is evaluated |
|---|---:|---|
| Implementation of the Assessments | 60 | Required mechanisms and scope implemented; correctness, meaningful tests, reproducible evidence, and ability to explain the work. Each assignment brief allocates these 60 points to its specific requirements. |
| [Frictional](prerequisites/frictional.md) | 10 | An honest, specific log of attempts, struggle, assistance, learning, and unresolved questions. |
| [GitHub version posting](prerequisites/github-submission.md) | 10 | A properly organized, accessible, identifiable version of the submission that matches Canvas. |
| [Relative Quartile](prerequisites/relative-quartile.md) | 20 | Overall quality relative to peers after all submissions are reviewed. |
| **Total** | **100** | |

There is no separate video score. An explainer video is optional and can support the quality of the work considered under Relative Quartile. Neither a video nor Brutalist is required for full credit.

The cadence is one Assignment every 10 days: deadline offsets are 10, 20, 30 days, and so on from the instructor-published course assignment anchor. Canvas publishes the actual calendar dates, covered Assessments, and final cutoff. Do not infer term dates or a fixed assignment count from the fifteen lesson folders.

## Submission

Upload to Canvas and post the matching version to GitHub under `fall-2025/first-name-last-initial/assignment-XX/`, for example `fall-2025/jane-d/assignment-01/`. Include the GitHub folder link and final commit hash with the Canvas upload. Follow the [GitHub posting rubric](prerequisites/github-submission.md).

Keep local practice in ignored `learning-artifacts/`; copy only the reviewed, sanitized submission into the tracked semester folder. Do not overwrite lesson reference solutions. Credit sources and distinguish human and Claude contributions; no artificial percentage estimate is needed.

Read the [AI policy](prerequisites/ai-policy.md). You must be able to explain submitted work. Do not submit secrets, restricted data, or invented results. Use Python and PEP 8.

## Optional explainers and capstone practice

The Week 8 and Week 15 Progress Reel ideas are optional explainer opportunities, not additional graded requirements in this structure. Use the [optional Brutalist guide](prerequisites/brutalist-video.md) or another workflow. Course films may be organized under [youtube/](youtube/README.md); no public YouTube upload is required.

The Week 15 capstone packet remains an ungraded Assessment integrating the earlier mechanisms. Its code, tests, fixtures, and packet can support a graded Assignment when its published brief includes that scope. The packet validator checks structure; it cannot establish factual correctness or authenticate approvals.

This assignment-level structure supersedes the earlier proposed lab point split. Other unresolved course-level grading and late-work rules remain in [instructor decisions](docs/instructor-decisions.md).
