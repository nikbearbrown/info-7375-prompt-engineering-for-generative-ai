# Blueprint risk assessment

Prepared after the author's approval of the chapter specifications. Proposed for final Blueprint review; not a research or manuscript sign-off.

## Positioning and comparable work

The immediate audience is INFO 7375 students who prefer sustained reading, with independent programmers as a secondary audience. The differentiator is a one-to-one narrative companion to runnable Claude/Python lessons: explain the machinery and judge its trade-offs rather than catalog tools.

The local examples inspected during Vision offer three editorial comparisons. AI1 CLI emphasizes artifact-led progression; this book retains that progression but teaches model-facing mechanisms. Trust the Teacher builds a sustained argument from a tension; this book uses inspectable technical tensions rather than borrowing its policy evidence. The Reallocation Engine separates fluent execution from judgment; this book makes that distinction concrete through controllers, tests, and authorization boundaries. Those examples are structural references, not verified factual authorities.

## Delivery risks and controls

- **Stale product details:** isolate changing setup and entitlement information in course prerequisites. Research records the date and primary source of API/protocol claims. Do not silently replace the course's Claude-only stack.
- **Invented narrative evidence:** label hypotheticals and computed examples. Do not fabricate Bear's memories, student experiences, stakeholder consent, or live model outcomes.
- **Borrowed authority:** companion-book arguments and empirical claims require source inspection. Omit generic closing notes. Preserve the difference between an argued position and an established result.
- **Assessment confusion:** all book Assessments remain ungraded. The ten-day 60/10/10/20 Assignment structure stays outside the narrative. Neither a video nor Brutalist becomes compulsory.
- **Visual scope creep:** Cajal specifies only figures that materially help explanation; no per-chapter image quota. Read the full applicable Cajal instructions before production, then audit geometry and substance separately.
- **Build-tool collisions:** do not run a broad AI1 sync over the course's contributor instructions, scripts, or existing files. Inspect the manifest and dependencies before choosing a minimal, isolated build integration. Exclude chapters/README.md and verification sidecars from EPUB assembly.
- **False completion:** human approval of a plan does not approve unwritten chapters. Preserve actual approvals, unresolved checks, and source records; never manufacture signed gates or claim a built EPUB has passed device review without evidence.

## Feature priorities

Required: fifteen one-to-one chapters; Teardown narrative; verified worked examples; Python/Claude boundaries; ungraded Assessments; selective source notes; purposeful Cajal illustrations; reflowable EPUB with navigation and static assets.

Helpful within scope: an HTML reading preview and an editorial report that checks narrative flow against the Blueprint.

Out of scope: a new website, Canvas export, new quiz bank, Anki deck, additional model providers, mandatory videos, KDP publication, market research campaign, paid image/API calls, or public release inferred from local book production.
