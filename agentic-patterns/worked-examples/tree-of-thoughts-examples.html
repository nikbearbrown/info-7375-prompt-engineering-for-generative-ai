A bounded Tree-of-Thoughts recipe

Using 3, 3, 8 and 8 exactly once, find an expression equal to 24 with +, −, ×, ÷ and parentheses. Track each candidate state and its input occurrences. Generate at most three next moves per retained state; keep at most two. Label heuristic ranks as judgments. With two values left, use exact arithmetic to test every final operation and prune impossible states. Limit depth to three operations, 15 candidate evaluations and 60 seconds. Verify complete expressions independently. Stop on a verified solution; otherwise return NO VERIFIED SOLUTION with explored states. Never claim exhaustive impossibility.

Illustrative teaching fixture. No live tool calls or agent run were executed. The runner should use exact fractions rather than floating-point tolerances and reject unsafe or non-arithmetic expression syntax. The initial heuristic ranking is illustrative; a real search could use an LLM, a domain heuristic or another value function. Deterministic search alone may be simpler for this tiny puzzle, which is chosen to teach the architecture.
