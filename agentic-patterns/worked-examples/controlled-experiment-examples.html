Replay checks the record, not the model

  • ValidationExactly five ordered rounds per match; two legal moves per round; one fixed payoff version.
  • Replay expectationRescoring the saved A–B moves gives 14 / 14.
  • Known-bad fixtureA stored total of 15 / 14 must be rejected.
  • LimitReplaying choices is deterministic. Resampling a model may change choices.
Illustrative teaching fixture. No agent workflow was run.7 / 10

The replay is described and arithmetically specified, not reported as an executed experimental run. Editorial arithmetic checking is distinct from an actual arena replay. A real deterministic scorer should recompute per-round payoffs rather than trust stored totals, validate round indices and reject missing, duplicate or invalid records. Pin a scoring version and record digest. Deterministic replay reproduces consequences of a recorded trace, not the stochastic generation process. The source tree provides no explicit check or stop rule; these are teaching additions grounded in best practices 6, 7 and 17.

Stop without rewriting the experiment

  • CompleteAll three matches contain five legal rounds; replayed scores match.
  • Invalid move or missing recordStop the affected match as INCOMPLETE; retain the failure.
  • Changed rules or scorerReject comparison; start a separately identified experiment.
  • Budget exhaustedStop at 15 rounds; never add trials to chase a preferred result.
Illustrative teaching fixture. No agent workflow was run.8 / 10

The completion and failure rules are explicitly added for this bounded example, not attributed to the source catalog. In a real live protocol, define tool timeout, model-call ceiling and invalid-output handling before the run; do not silently repair an invalid choice or cherry-pick successful matches. A failed match is part of the data. A changed payoff matrix yields a new scoring question and cannot be substituted into an old outcome claim.

A bounded arena prompt

In an isolated arena, compare A against B, Always-C and Always-D for exactly five rounds per match. Freeze prompts, model versions, rules and payoff matrix before starting. Expose only past rounds; accept one C or D from each player, then reveal both. Reset history between matches. Append every move and payoff; never edit prior records or the scorer. Stop at 15 rounds total. Invalid output, missing evidence or a rule change ends that match as INCOMPLETE. Recompute scores from saved moves and report limits. Use supplied fixtures for this demonstration; make no claims about actual model behavior.
Illustrative teaching fixture. No agent workflow was run.9 / 10

This is a reusable bounded protocol, not an experiment executed here. For a real run, replace fixtures with authorized model calls and identify exact models, decoding settings, prompt version, harness version, information policy, call/time/cost caps and independent scorer version. Model sampling and tool availability can affect results even under a fixed seed; repeat trials and disclose that uncertainty. Do not extrapolate from the teaching fixture.
