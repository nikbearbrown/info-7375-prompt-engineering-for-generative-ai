A bounded exit-gate prompt

Inspect release candidate C7 read-only against the fixed identity, regression, staging-freshness and rollback rubric. Bind every artifact to the exact candidate and package; reject stale or mismatched reports. Do not repair, merge, deploy or approve. Cap at ten minutes and one re-fetch per missing artifact. Return GO only for satisfied gates, BLOCK for known failures, or CANNOT_VERIFY for missing evidence. Write a restartable handoff covering state, evidence, risks, untouched scope and one safe next action. Name what was not checked. Return control to the release owner and stop, including after GO.
