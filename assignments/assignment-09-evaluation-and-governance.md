# Assignment 09 Evaluation and Governance

**Due:** Canvas · **Points:** 100 · **Assessments:** [Lesson 13](../lessons/13-evaluation-and-approval-gates/docs/en.md) and [Lesson 14](../lessons/14-team-governance-and-ethics/docs/en.md)

Read the [course AI policy](../prerequisites/ai-policy.md) and watch [AI Policy for Professor Bear's Courses | Using AI Responsibly in Class](https://youtu.be/8Ut0Cdl6vMw?si=9w3aEpt1ZyAR4Kiz).

## Implementation 60 points

- Implement canonical proposal fingerprints and decisions bound to one exact proposal. **15 points**
- Test changed targets, changed effects, expired or absent decisions, and valid approval lookup. **12 points**
- Design a reviewable gate naming action, target, reason, effects, evidence, rollback, identity, and authority. **12 points**
- Create a team AI-use register entry with owner, purpose, data classes, retention, evaluation, approver, and incident contact. **12 points**
- Define a bounded incident path with named pause authority and a stop when authority is unresolved. **9 points**

## Submit

Ship the approval implementation, tests, gate design, governance register, and incident response. Include [Frictional](../prerequisites/frictional.md) **10 points**, [GitHub posting](../prerequisites/github-submission.md) **10 points**, and [Relative Quartile](../prerequisites/relative-quartile.md) **20 points**. An explainer video is optional.
