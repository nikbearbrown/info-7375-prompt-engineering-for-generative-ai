# Assignment 10 Supervised Agentic Capstone

**Due:** Canvas · **Points:** 100 · **Assessment:** [Lesson 15](../lessons/15-supervised-capstone/docs/en.md)

Read the [course AI policy](../prerequisites/ai-policy.md) and watch [AI Policy for Professor Bear's Courses | Using AI Responsibly in Class](https://youtu.be/8Ut0Cdl6vMw?si=9w3aEpt1ZyAR4Kiz).

## Implementation 60 points

- Define a real approved need, named owner, data boundary, action surface, and success evidence. **10 points**
- Produce the plan, dependencies, permissions, budgets, stop conditions, approval gates, and premortem. **12 points**
- Implement the bounded Python mechanism and meaningful tests required by the project. **14 points**
- Execute or simulate only as authorized; preserve evidence and label every simulation, fixture, and unverified claim. **10 points**
- Deliver the ten-artifact packet: brief, data boundary, action-surface map, plan, gates, premortem, evidence, artifact, audit note, and transfer reflection. **10 points**
- Record the final human decision and handoff without treating packet completeness as approval. **4 points**

## Submit

Ship the complete supervised capstone packet and reproducible repository version. Include [Frictional](../prerequisites/frictional.md) **10 points**, [GitHub posting](../prerequisites/github-submission.md) **10 points**, and [Relative Quartile](../prerequisites/relative-quartile.md) **20 points**. An explainer video is optional and public posting is not required.
