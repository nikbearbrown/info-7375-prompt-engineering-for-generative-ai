# Assignment 06 Planning Before Acting

**Due:** Canvas · **Points:** 100 · **Assessment:** [Lesson 10](../lessons/10-planning-before-acting/docs/en.md)

Read the [course AI policy](../prerequisites/ai-policy.md) and watch [AI Policy for Professor Bear's Courses | Using AI Responsibly in Class](https://youtu.be/8Ut0Cdl6vMw?si=9w3aEpt1ZyAR4Kiz).

## Implementation 60 points

- Define a bounded objective, deliverable, constraints, evidence, permissions, dependencies, budget, and stop conditions. **16 points**
- Implement plan validation and dependency ordering in Python. **14 points**
- Test missing fields, cycles, unauthorized actions, meaningless placeholder content, and valid plans. **14 points**
- Ask Claude to critique the plan, then document what you accepted, revised, or rejected. **8 points**
- Produce the human-reviewed execution plan without executing consequential actions. **8 points**

## Submit

Ship the reviewed plan, validator, tests, critique record, and approval status. Include [Frictional](../prerequisites/frictional.md) **10 points**, [GitHub posting](../prerequisites/github-submission.md) **10 points**, and [Relative Quartile](../prerequisites/relative-quartile.md) **20 points**. An explainer video is optional.
